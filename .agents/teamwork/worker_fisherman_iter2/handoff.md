## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Kinematics Orientation Fix**:
   - `walkStep(S, pts, a0, a1, stop)` computes `w.a` and returns `{ P: frameAt(w.i, w.j, a), walk, g, calm }`.
   - `frameAt` creates `{ i, j, fi: Math.cos(a), fj: Math.sin(a), z0 }`.
   - By assigning `w.P.z0 = DECK_Z; P = w.P;`, the valid trigonometric heading vectors `fi` and `fj` are directly preserved.
   - This eliminates `Math.cos(undefined) = NaN` and guarantees all joint transformations in `toWorld` and `figureHD` receive finite numerical inputs.
   - Confirmed by zero NaN values across 100% of ticks in `scratch/adversarial_fisherman_stress.mjs` and `scratch/adversarial_probe.mjs`.

2. **Obstacle Collision Avoidance**:
   - Tackle box is located at `[49.87, 23.55, 50.43, 23.91]`.
   - The path `[49.60, 24.05] -> [49.40, 23.50] -> [49.40, 21.80] -> [51.20, 21.80]` maintains `i <= 49.60` until `j = 21.80`, which is well clear of the box's boundaries.
   - Sampling every point along the path confirmed 0 blocked samples against `districtBlocked`.
   - Crate footprint is `[45.0, 20.2, 45.8, 21.0]`. Taking off from `[46.10, 21.23]` and landing at `[46.00, 21.06]` ensures that whenever the cat is horizontally within the crate's footprint, it is in mid-air (`z >= DECK_Z + 0.5`), completely eliminating ground-level interior penetration.

3. **Deterministic Inventory Triggering**:
   - `BUCKET_DROP_TICK` (1441) and `BUCKET_STEAL_TICK` (1752) are explicitly bound to the step phase definitions of `FISH` and `CAT`.
